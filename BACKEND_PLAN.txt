HOUSE OF KANISHKA – BACKEND BUILD PLAN
Final version: 15 November 2026. Work starts 16 November 2026.
====================================================================


1. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Database and storage   Supabase (PostgreSQL + Storage), region Mumbai (ap-south-1)
Server code            Next.js Route Handlers (app/api/*) and Server Actions, in TypeScript
Accounts               Clerk (already built), connected to Supabase as a
                       third-party sign-in provider, so Supabase's access rules
                       (row-level security) know who each customer is
Payments               Razorpay with our own checkout form: the form is ours, and Razorpay's
                       own payment window takes the card/UPI details
                       (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets) + Cash on Delivery
Order emails           Resend + React Email (sent from our own domain)
Newsletter             Brevo (free plan), with double opt-in
Shipping               Shiprocket (Lite plan), tracking page we build ourselves(LATER NOT NOW)
Bill / invoice PDF     @react-pdf/renderer, saved in Supabase Storage
Search                 Postgres full-text search + pg_trgm (for typo tolerance)
Spam protection        Cloudflare Turnstile + rate limits
Hosting                Vercel free (Hobby) plan
Budget                 Free plans only. No paid plans for now.
Domain                 Bought.will use after backend finishes.


2. BUSINESS ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
GST registered         NO. Issue a "bill of supply" (no tax lines). Ready to switch to
                       GST invoices if the business registers later.
                       Check with an accountant (CA): selling goods to customers in other states
                       can require GST registration whatever the turnover.
Cash on Delivery       YES. The customer confirms the order by a free email link. The
                       COD order limit and COD on/off are admin settings.
Free shipping from     Rs 350 (the current code says Rs 35,000, which was a mistake). With
                       every product above Rs 350, every order ships free.
                       The fee for smaller orders is an admin setting.
Shipping area          India only.
Returns                7 days, refunded to the original payment method
                       (COD orders: to the customer's bank account or UPI ID).
Business details       Placeholder details for now, clearly marked so they are easy to replace.
Admin users            Founder only. The admin area must be hidden.


3. DESIGN AND MOTION RULES (apply to EVERY phase)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Every new screen, state, form, email and document must look like the
current site and feel luxurious and premium.

Look
- Colours only from the design tokens in spec section 12.2
  (--vr-red #A20F15, black, white, greys).
- The site's existing serif, script and sans fonts and type sizes.
  Square corners everywhere.
- Reuse the existing components: Eyebrow, UnderlineLink, btn/field
  classes, Drawer, Breadcrumbs, Emblem (lotus), Handwriting, RiseWords,
  RevealObserver, the skeleton loaders, the error/offline page styles.
  Anything new must be designed to match them.

Motion: luxurious, advanced, with a premium feel
- Covers payment, checkout, invoice, shipping/tracking pages, customer
  forms, the account area and the admin area.
- Extends the existing motion: lotus bloom, handwritten
  script, rising words, scroll reveals, soft fades.
- Premium = smooth and deliberate: slow, expressive easing, layered
  timing (headline, then details, then actions), content revealed through masks,
  numbers counting up, smooth transitions between screens, small
  responses to hover and focus.
- Animate only position, scale and opacity (CSS transform and opacity),
  smooth (60 fps) on mid-range phones. Every animation can be
  interrupted, never blocks a click, and long sequences can be skipped.
- Visitors who turn off motion in their device settings (prefers-reduced-motion)
  get the finished state straight away.
- Existing spec pages keep their current motion. New motion is for new screens
  and states only.

Checks before a phase counts as done
- Luxury motion rules applied while building, motion QA afterwards.
- Screenshots at 1440 px and 393 px, compared with the existing pages.

Outside the site
- Emails and the PDF bill use the site's fonts, red and the lotus
  (no animation, because emails and PDFs can't animate).
- The Razorpay payment window can only take the brand red and our logo; our own
  animation before and after it hides the handover.


4. THE PHASES
====================================================================

PHASE 0 – SETUP AND POLICY PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Supabase project (Mumbai) connected to Clerk as a third-party sign-in provider.
- Supabase CLI: database migrations, generated TypeScript types,
  and a database connection that only runs on the server.
- Startup check for environment variables (zod), updated .env.example.
- A cloudflared tunnel so webhooks (Clerk, Razorpay, Shiprocket) reach this computer while testing.
- GitHub Actions: a ping every few days so the free database doesn't pause, plus
  a nightly database backup.
- Change the free-shipping limit from Rs 35,000 to Rs 350 (lib/products.ts,
  the free-shipping bar, the promo text).
- New pages: Terms, Privacy, Refund (7 days), Shipping (India only),
  Contact (placeholder business details). Linked from the footer. Razorpay
  requires these pages before it activates the account.
- Motion: headings rise in, text reveals as you scroll, a sticky contents
  list highlights the current section, the existing underline effect on links.

PHASE 1 – PRODUCTS IN THE DATABASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tables: products, variants (size x colour), inventory, collections,
  lookbook looks and hotspots, homepage content. Access rules (row-level security)
  on every table.
- Move the data from lib/products.ts, lib/collections.ts and lib/lookbook.ts
  into the database.
- Pages read from Supabase, cached, and refreshed whenever a product changes.
- Search moves to Postgres (keeping the typo tolerance and suggestions).
- Placeholder photos stay local only (they must never be published).
- Pages must look and move exactly as they do now (screenshot comparison).
- Motion: sold-out sizes fade with a fine strike-through, and "few left" fades in.

Launch scope: DRESSES ONLY. Accessories launch later as a "coming soon" teaser
- Accessories stay in the database. Each product has a status: draft / preview / live
  (preview = only the founder sees it on the real site). Each category has a launch switch:
  Accessories = "coming soon".
- While coming soon:
  * The homepage Accessories section and /accessories keep their current design,
    but show "Accessories – coming soon" and a "Notify me" email sign-up instead of
    prices and buttons. Sign-ups go to a launch-waitlist table (with consent) and Brevo.
  * Accessory product pages are not public (404), and are left out of search, suggestions,
    the sitemap and New Arrivals.
  * Collections (e.g. ASH X COS: wool beret, silk scarf) and lookbook hotspots
    leave accessories out or show them as "coming soon" (not clickable).
  * The server refuses accessories in a bag, wishlist or checkout (also covers bags
    saved earlier).
- At launch: the founder flips the switch in the admin area, everything goes live together,
  pages refresh straight away, and the waitlist gets a launch email.
- Motion: the coming-soon state uses the site's motion style (soft fade-in of the text,
  the lotus blooms when someone signs up for "Notify me").

PHASE 2 – CUSTOMERS, ACCOUNT AREA, LOGIN, FORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Backend
- Clerk webhook copies each account into a profiles table (and anonymises it when the account is deleted).
- Saved addresses, a consent record (who agreed to what, and when).
- Bag and wishlist saved to the account. A guest's bag is merged in
  at sign-in.
- Newsletter: Brevo with double opt-in, plus our own subscriber table.
- Contact form: saved in Supabase and emailed to us, with Turnstile and rate limits.
- Customers can export their data and delete their account (required by India's DPDP Act).

Login page
- After signing in, the customer returns to the page they came from (checkout or product
  page). Only addresses on our own site are allowed. The default is /account.
- "Sign in for faster checkout" at checkout (guests can still check out without an account).
- Add sign-in by email code (free in Clerk) alongside password and Google.
- Motion after signing in: the form fades out, the lotus blooms, and "Welcome back" is
  written in script before moving on.

Account area (currently /account shows settings only)
- /account               Overview: handwritten "Welcome back, [name]", the latest
                         order and how far it has got, quick links
- /account/addresses     Saved addresses, default address
- /account/wishlist      The saved wishlist
- /account/preferences   Newsletter and marketing consent
- /account/settings      Today's account page, moved here unchanged
                         (profile, email, password, devices, sign out, delete)
- Navigation: serif section links, a left column on desktop, scrollable tabs on
  mobile, and a red line that slides to the current section.

Motion for every customer form (login, account, addresses, newsletter, contact)
- Labels float up, a fine red line draws under the field being typed in,
  error messages slide down gently (no shaking), buttons show the lotus while busy,
  success shows a lotus bloom with handwritten thanks. Saved addresses
  rearrange smoothly when one is added or removed.

PHASE 3 – CHECKOUT, PAYMENTS, CASH ON DELIVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- The server checks every field: contact details, phone (required), address, India PIN code.
- Prices are re-checked against the database. Stock is held for 15 minutes and
  released by Supabase pg_cron (the free Vercel plan only runs scheduled jobs once a day).
- Shipping: free from Rs 350, with the fee for smaller orders set in the admin area.
- Online payment: the server creates a Razorpay order, Razorpay's payment window
  opens (brand red, our logo, customer details already filled in), the server checks
  the payment signature, and the payment.captured webhook is the final word.
  Each order is processed only once, even if the webhook arrives more than once.
- Cash on delivery: the order is marked "awaiting confirmation" and
  the customer confirms it by an email link. COD order limit and on/off in the admin area.
- Everything is built and tested in Razorpay test mode.
- Motion: checkout steps (Contact, Delivery, Payment) fold into
  summaries once done; the order total counts up; the chosen payment method
  expands; a white veil with the lotus appears before the Razorpay window opens;
  "Confirming payment" shows the lotus opening. Smooth recovery screens
  when the payment window is closed, the payment fails, a retry is needed, or
  the customer comes back from a UPI app.

PHASE 4 – ORDERS, BILL OF SUPPLY, EMAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Order lifecycle (placed, paid or COD-confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered,
  refunded) and order numbers.
- Bill of supply PDF (no GST), saved in Storage, with placeholder business details.
- Order emails through Resend from our own domain (confirmation, shipped,
  delivered, refund), with DNS records set up for our domain.
- Account: /account/orders and /account/orders/[id], with a bill download.
- Confirmation page animation: the lotus opens, a paper sheet slides up, the
  lines rise one by one, the amounts count up, the total is written in script,
  and a red lotus stamp ("Paid", or "To pay on delivery" for COD)
  presses down; then the action buttons fade in. It plays once and can be skipped,
  and shows the finished invoice for visitors who turn motion off.
- Motion: orders in the account expand to show their details; opening the bill moves
  smoothly into its preview.

PHASE 6 – HIDDEN ADMIN AREA (FOUNDER ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Access: only the founder's Clerk user ID (stored as an environment variable).
  Everyone else, even signed-in customers, sees the normal 404 page.
- Never linked from the site, left out of the sitemap, marked noindex, at a less
  obvious address than /admin. Every action is checked again on the server and logged.
- Category launch switches (Accessories: coming soon / live), product status
  (draft / preview / live), and the launch waitlist with a "send launch email" button.
- Manage: products, stock, orders (including confirming COD), refunds,
  shipping fee, COD settings, homepage content, newsletter subscribers,
  contact messages. Sales report as a spreadsheet (CSV), low-stock emails.
- Motion: smooth page changes, key numbers count up, charts draw
  themselves in, table rows appear one by one, smooth reordering when sorting or
  filtering, side panels for editing, an instant red check when something is saved,
  a gentle confirmation panel for risky actions. Every animation is short and can be
  interrupted, so it never slows down the work.

PHASE 7 – CHECKS AND LAUNCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tests that the access rules hold (no customer can see another customer's data), plus a security review.
- Sentry (error tracking), UptimeRobot (downtime alerts), analytics only after cookie consent.
- Test that a backup can actually be restored.
- End-to-end Playwright tests: a paid order and a COD order.
- Screenshot comparison of every new page at 1440 px and 393 px, and motion QA
  on every new animation (smoothness, loading speed, reduced motion, real phones).
- Razorpay account check (KYC), live payment keys, email DNS on our domain.
- Hosting stays on the free Vercel plan for now.
